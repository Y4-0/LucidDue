# LucidDue

> A lightweight, secure SaaS dashboard for freelancers to track outstanding invoices and send professional follow-ups. It answers exactly what needs your attention right now, without the overhead of traditional accounting software.

## 🚀 Overview

**Track invoices. Send follow-ups. Get paid.**

Ditch the heavy accounting software. LucidDue is a minimal, secure dashboard that tells you exactly who owes you money and helps you follow up with a single click. Minimum data entry, maximum peace of mind.

## 🏗️ Architecture & Stack

A secure, minimal SaaS application for invoice tracking and follow-ups. Built with a Next.js frontend, a NestJS/PostgreSQL/Prisma backend, and strictly enforced server-side authorization. Designed around the principle of minimal data entry and clear actionable dashboards (Track → Understand → Remind → Record).

## 💡 Our Philosophy

LucidDue was built for freelancers and small businesses who need to manage their cash flow but don't want to become part-time accountants. We believe in strong visual hierarchy, zero clutter, and making sure the most important question is always answered: *"What needs my attention right now?"* By pairing minimal data entry with smart, professional templates, we help you maintain healthy client relationships and get paid on time.
